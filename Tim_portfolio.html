Serpent

Live
Problem

After a snake sighting or bite, people often guess wrong about whether the snake is dangerous — and a confident wrong answer can cost a life.

What I built

A safety-first snake identification and venom-risk web app: upload one photo and it names the closest-matching species out of 4,245, or says Unknown when it can’t be sure.

  • AI vision identification behind a swappable provider layer (OpenAI or Claude)
  • English, Thai and Burmese support, with species names kept untranslated
  • Emergency “I’ve been bitten” flow, species browser, and private on-device result history
